What you can do

One platform for people who walk the city and the teams who maintain it.

Compare routes

See lighting, footpath, crossing and accessibility evidence for each route, with the number of observations behind every score.

Report street issues

Report poor lighting, damaged footpaths, blocked walkways, unsafe crossings and accessibility barriers, with a photo if you like.

AI-assisted review

AI sorts and summarises reports and flags possible duplicates. It only suggests. People verify.

City analytics

Admins see repeated problem areas, ward-level numbers, and what has been verified or resolved.

How it works

From a problem on the street to a fix, with the evidence in one place.

Citizen reportsA problem is reported with a location and description.
AI organisesThe report gets a category, summary and duplicate check.
City reviewsAn admin verifies it and sees patterns by ward.
Status is trackedEveryone can see when it is pending, verified or resolved.

Honest by design

We show the evidence behind every score. If there is not enough data, we say "Limited data available". Sample data in the demo is always labelled.
